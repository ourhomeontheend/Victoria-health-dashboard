Magnesium malate — take daily: Despite being an energy supplement, malate supports mitochondrial function constantly, not just on training days. On rest days it helps recovery from the previous session and maintains energy levels. Slightly lower dose on rest days is fine but daily is better than sporadic.
Methylated B complex: Regular B vitamins need converting by your body. A common genetic variant (MTHFR) makes this inefficient for many people. Methylated versions (methylfolate + methylcobalamin) are already in the active form — better absorbed by everyone. Good brands: Thorne, Seeking Health, Jarrow. Take in the morning as B vitamins are energising.
